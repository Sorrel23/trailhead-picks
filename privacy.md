Last updated: November 15, 2026

Trailhead Picks is a static website. We do not run accounts, comments or forms, and we do not collect personal information directly.

**Amazon links.** Product links go to Amazon.com and carry our Amazon Associates tracking ID. When you click one, Amazon may set cookies to credit the referral. Amazon's privacy notice governs that data.

**Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data (such as IP address) for security and operations under GitHub's privacy statement.

**Pinterest.** We publish our own pins to our own Pinterest account using Pinterest's API. We do not access, store or share other Pinterest users' data.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Sorrel23/trailhead-picks.
