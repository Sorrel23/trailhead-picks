## How to choose your first pair

Most new hikers do best with **telescoping poles with lever locks**. Lever (flick) locks are easy to adjust with cold or tired hands and simple to tighten. **Folding poles** pack shorter and stash in a pack or suitcase, but usually adjust over a smaller range. **Aluminum** bends before it breaks and tends to cost less; **carbon** is lighter but can snap under a hard sideways load. Grips matter too: cork handles sweat well, foam is soft and light.

## Sizing tip for first-timers

Stand in hiking shoes with the pole tip on the ground and adjust until your elbow sits near a right angle. Shorten a little for climbs and lengthen for descents. If you're giving poles as a gift, choose an adjustable pair so the recipient can dial in their own fit.
