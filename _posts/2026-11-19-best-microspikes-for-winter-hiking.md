## How to choose winter traction

Match the device to the terrain. **Coils and studs** (Yaktrax, NANOspikes) are for sidewalks, flat paths and patchy ice. **Microspikes** with roughly 3/8-inch spikes handle most winter hiking trails, including rolling hills of packed snow and ice. **Longer-spike "trail crampons"** grip better on steep, hard snow but feel clunky on rock and pavement. None of these replace real crampons on steep alpine ice.

Size for the footwear they'll actually wear in winter. Insulated winter boots are often bulkier than summer hikers, so check the brand's size chart against the boot, not just the shoe size.

## The short version

For most winter hikers, the Kahtoola MICROspikes are the one to get. Choose the Black Diamond Distance Spike if light weight and easy on-and-off matter most, the Hillsound Trail Crampon for steep packed snow, and NANOspikes or Yaktrax for town.
