Shopping for an outdoorsy dad is tricky. He probably owns a tent, a backpack and a cooler already, and he tends to say he doesn't need anything. The best outdoor gifts for dad are the ones that upgrade something he does every trip: making coffee, lighting up camp, sitting by the fire, fixing small things, and getting home safe.

We built this list from Outdoor Life's 2026 gifts-for-dads guide, GearJunkie's outdoor gift picks for dads and owner reviews. We skipped hunting rifles and other specialist gear that's hard to buy for someone else, and kept to things that work for a camping, hiking or backyard-fire kind of dad.

## Gifts for around camp

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
### 1. Jetboil Flash Java — coffee at the campsite, fast

**Why it works:** This is Jetboil's quick-boiling Flash stove with a silicone coffee press that packs inside the pot. GearJunkie points out the newer valve turns on with a half-turn of the knob and the igniter is sturdier. For a dad who wakes up first at camp, it turns the morning routine into a two-minute job.

**Skip if:** he already loves his camp stove, or he cooks real meals outside. A tall, narrow boil-pot is made for water, not frying eggs.

**Buying tip:** It runs on screw-on fuel canisters, so check whether one is included and plan to grab one locally if not. A bag of his favorite coffee makes it a complete gift.

### 2. Crazy Creek Hex 2.0 Original Chair — a seat anywhere

**Why it works:** It's a folding foam seat with a backrest held up by straps, so it works on a rock, a tent floor, a beach or a stadium bench. GearJunkie notes it rolls up to about four inches across and uses carbon fiber stays and closed-cell foam. It's light enough that he'll actually bring it.

**Skip if:** he wants a tall camp chair with legs and a cup holder for car camping. This sits on the ground.

**Buying tip:** It's a smart pick for a dad who hikes or canoes to camp, where a full chair is too bulky.

### 3. Dometic Unrestricted 17L cooler backpack — cold drinks, hands free

**Why it works:** GearJunkie stresses that it's a real backpack, not a cooler with straps added. It holds up to 28 cans, has a leakproof liner and PrimaLoft insulation, and even has a laptop sleeve. Think lake walks and tailgates.

**Skip if:** he needs to keep food cold for a multi-day trip. A 17-liter soft pack is a day cooler, not a replacement for his big hard cooler.

**Buying tip:** Pre-chill it and use frozen water bottles instead of loose ice. They keep things cold and become drinking water later.

## Gifts for the campfire

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
### 5. Council Tool Flying Fox hatchet — for splitting kindling

**Why it works:** Outdoor Life picks this American-made hatchet as a fun, well-balanced tool. At camp or at home, a good hatchet makes quick work of splitting kindling for the fire, and it's a nicer tool than the cheap ones that come in camping kits.

**Skip if:** he only burns store-bought bundles that are already split small, or he camps where fires are often banned.

**Buying tip:** A hatchet is sharp from the factory, so make sure it ships with a cover or add a leather sheath. Pair it with a pair of leather work gloves.

{% include amzn.html asin="B07ZHL52LW" text="Check the Council Tool Flying Fox on Amazon" %}
</div>

<div class="pick" markdown="1">
### 6. White Duck canvas firewood log carrier — a small, handsome upgrade

**Why it works:** GearJunkie likes this waxed-canvas log carrier with reinforced web handles and a hook-and-loop closure. It saves his arms, keeps bark and dirt off his shirt, and looks good by a wood stove or fire pit between trips.

**Skip if:** he doesn't have a fire pit, wood stove or fireplace and rarely camps with a fire.

**Buying tip:** This is a good gift under a tree because it's hard to get wrong. Tuck a box of long matches or a fire starter inside.

## Gifts for the trail

**Buying tip:** It's a great "everyone needs one" gift. Dads use headlamps for car repairs, power outages and dog walks as much as for camping.

**Buying tip:** Leatherman sells several Wave versions, so check the listing says Wave Alpha. It makes a good pairing with the headlamp for a truck or camp kit.

## How to pick the right outdoor gift for dad

Think about what he does most. A car-camping dad will love the cooler backpack, the Thermacell and the log carrier. A hiking dad gets more out of the headlamp, the Crazy Creek seat and the inReach. If you're stuck, a headlamp or a multi-tool is almost never a miss.
